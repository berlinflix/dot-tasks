Dot · Privacy policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Dot is a to-do app built so that your tasks stay private. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Your tasks are end-to-end encrypted

Task titles, notes, lists, dates and reminder times are encrypted on your phone before they are uploaded. The cloud copy (Google Firebase, Mumbai region) is ciphertext that neither we nor Google can read. The key is held by you: on your phone, in an end-to-end encrypted Android Block Store backup protected by your screen lock, and as a recovery key that only you have.

What is collected

  • Account: when you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account id, email address and name, solely to identify your encrypted data and let you sign in.
  • Encrypted task data: stored only so it can sync between your devices and survive reinstalling the app. The server also sees when records change and their approximate size.
  • App integrity: Firebase App Check uses Google Play Integrity to confirm requests come from the genuine app.

Dot contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not sell or share data.

Voice input

The microphone is used only while you add a task by voice. Speech is converted to text by Android's on-device speech recognizer; the app never records audio to storage and never sends audio anywhere.

On your phone

The local database is encrypted (SQLCipher) with a key protected by the Android Keystore. App data is excluded from Android backups.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This deletes your encrypted cloud data, the key backup and your account. You can also use the web deletion page. Signing out removes all tasks from the phone.
